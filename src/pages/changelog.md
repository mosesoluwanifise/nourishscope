# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — October 2026

First public release.

- Planning tool with three modules: Nutrition Program Planning, Community Health Context, and Food System and Access.
- Five allocation strategies compared side by side, with cost sensitivity and equity flags for rural and economically distressed counties.
- Data options: Kentucky county data, labelled sample data, and CSV upload matched to Kentucky counties.
- Evaluation module for before-and-after program results.
- Planning brief as a printable PDF and a CSV export.
- Methodology version 1.0.

### Data releases in dataset v1.0

- U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 5-Year Estimates, 2020–2024.
- CDC PLACES County Data, 2024 release (2022 diabetes and obesity; 2021 high blood pressure).
- USDA ERS Food Environment Atlas, 2025 data download.
- Appalachian Regional Commission, County Economic Status, Fiscal Year 2027.
