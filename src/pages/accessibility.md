# Accessibility

NourishScope aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, so that it works with a keyboard, screen readers, zoom and phones.

What we have done:

- Every page except the planning tool works without JavaScript.
- Every form field has a visible label, and error messages appear next to the field they are about.
- Charts always have a table with the same numbers, and never rely on colour alone.
- Changes to results are announced to screen readers.
- Text can be enlarged to 200%, and pages work at phone width without sideways scrolling.

If something does not work for you, please tell us through the [Contact](/contact) page, including the page and what you were trying to do. We will try to fix it, and to give you the information another way in the meantime.
